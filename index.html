F A L C O · the geometric arcana

Twenty Keys, drawn from the old French trumps and dressed in commedia dell'arte — each one a spinning top built from a real tessellation, and each tessellation is a working piece of math or computing you can test with your own hands.

Tap any Key to stop its spin and turn it over. The back is the truth beneath the mask — the geometry, and why it matters.

Key I — Il Bagatto, the Router

The Bagatto is the trickster who moves things faster than the eye can follow. His geometry is a systolic pipeline: a lattice of little processors, each holding a piece of the answer in its own memory cell and passing it on. Below are two ways to add two 4-bit numbers. Ripple-carry solves each digit in order, waiting on the one before it. Carry-lookahead solves every digit's carry at once, in parallel, across the same tessellated grid. Same answer — very different throughput.

The remaining nineteen Keys

Every card below is a real tessellation — hex lattices, phyllotaxis spirals, Sierpiński recursion, Voronoi mosaics, Truchet mazes, aperiodic tilings — standing in for a math or computing principle the commedia character embodies. A handful let you test the principle yourself; the rest simply show their work.
